Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Developer: MD SHARIK

This policy explains what information the Street Kings mobile game ("the game") handles and why. The game has no user accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (in-game cash, owned vehicles, paint and outfit choices) and settings (graphics quality, camera, music and vibration options) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares the following with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about how you interact with ads;
  • diagnostic information such as crash logs and app performance data.

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the first time you open the game you are asked for consent to personalised advertising. You can change it at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: they only play when you tap a "Watch ad" button.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback during play (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads SDK as described above.

Children

The game contains cartoon crime, police chases and weapons and is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected through ads, contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the current version.
